Tap Duel — 15-second on-chain tap battles

A real-time 1v1 clicker game on Solana Ephemeral Rollups. Fifteen seconds, as many taps as you can land.

A tap duel in 3D: two pads, one bolt of speed between them, and sparks for every tap. Drag to turn it.

↓ Scroll to explore

The problem

Fast tap battles do not fit slow, costly on-chain actions. If every tap needed a normal transaction, the game would stall and each round would cost fees.

The solution

Tap Duel runs 15-second 1v1 tap rounds on Solana using MagicBlock Ephemeral Rollups, with a built-in burner wallet so a player can start without signing every tap by hand.

The outcome

Two players tap against the same counter in real time, the round ends on time, and the score stands on-chain — played on Solana Devnet.
